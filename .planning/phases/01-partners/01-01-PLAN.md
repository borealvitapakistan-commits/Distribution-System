# Partners navigation and manufacturer deletion

## Objective
Group partner records in an animated sidebar menu and let owners delete unused manufacturers.

## Context
- Django application with owner-only master data and a shared sidebar.
- Partner links currently live under Settings.
- Manufacturer references use PROTECT for products, package pricing, and orders.
- Follow existing owner permissions, service transactions, audit events, and confirmation forms.

## Tasks
1. Move Manufacturers, Owners, Brand, and Distributors into a collapsible Partners sidebar menu. Animate expansion/collapse, collapse after selection, retain active styling, and support keyboard/reduced motion.
2. Add confirmed, POST-only manufacturer deletion from list/detail pages. Preserve linked records, show a useful error, audit successful deletion, and verify permission and data integrity behavior.

## Success criteria
- Partners reveals four working links and collapses after selecting one.
- Partner pages highlight the Partners section.
- An owner can delete an unused manufacturer after confirmation.
- Linked manufacturers cannot be deleted; no related data is lost.
- GET, anonymous, and distributor requests cannot delete records.
- Relevant Django tests and browser checks pass.
