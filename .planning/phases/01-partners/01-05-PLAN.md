# Manufacturer dropdowns and product prefixes

## Objective
Organize Products under compact, full-width animated manufacturer headers and display product names with an editable manufacturer prefix.

## Context
- User wants this categorization step before further pricing and PO work.
- Products can link to a primary manufacturer or several manufacturers through package prices.
- Preserve product/stock history and avoid duplicating or silently splitting existing records.
- Clarification requested about keeping shared records versus introducing independent manufacturer product records.

## Tasks
1. Add manufacturer prefixes, editable through existing screens/API, and tested manufacturer/product association helpers.
2. Add accessible manufacturer accordions, complete per-manufacturer product paging, contextual product labels, and creation/assignment controls; verify desktop/mobile behavior and regressions.

## Success criteria
- One compact full-width dropdown per manufacturer, including empty manufacturers.
- Shared products appear under each linked manufacturer without duplicate rows within a group or inflated inventory.
- Prefix example: Ashwagandha (GP); prefix is editable under Partners > Manufacturers.
- Products without a manufacturer remain accessible.
- Search/status filters, selection/actions, product editing, and stock history continue to work.
- No destructive conversion of existing shared product records without the architectural decision.
