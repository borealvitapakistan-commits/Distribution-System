# Remove manufacturer payment-before-shipping terms

## Objective
Remove the preset payment-before-shipping percentage from manufacturing screens.

## Context
- The manufacturer form exposes a preset payment percentage.
- The order advance page displays that preset and suggests its calculated amount.
- Existing order payments remain recorded; per-order advance entry continues.

## Tasks
1. Remove the manufacturer percentage from create/edit/admin screens and remove preset suggestions/default amounts from the advance page.
2. Verify manufacturer/payment screens and existing payment flow tests; record the result.

## Success criteria
- Manufacturer screens no longer show the payment-before-shipping term.
- Order advances start without a preset amount and accept the owner's per-order choice.
- Existing payment flow checks pass; no database migration needed.
