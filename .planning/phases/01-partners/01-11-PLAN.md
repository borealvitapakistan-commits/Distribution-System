# One business workbook, one import

## Objective
Deliver a single Excel workbook containing the real business sections and import all its sheets together. Keep full backups private and separate; business imports preserve owner login accounts and system configuration.

## Tasks
1. Add a business-only workbook mode with named category sheets, readable linked names and hidden stable identifiers/supporting attachment data. Import this mode through the existing atomic backup/replacement service while preserving accounts/system records.
2. Update Settings, verify full and business transfer paths on isolated databases, generate one local-business workbook, and restart the Supabase app without importing live data.

## Success criteria
One .xlsx upload imports all business sections together. No user/password/permission/audit/cache sheets in the downloadable business file. Related items/prices/orders remain linked. Original data and dated backups are preserved on failure. No live imports or migrations.
