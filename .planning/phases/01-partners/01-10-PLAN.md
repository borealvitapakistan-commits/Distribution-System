# Readable, editable category workbooks

## Objective
Replace the primary JSON-in-CSV transfer format with an Excel workbook: a named sheet per category, serial numbers and individual readable field columns. Use the same workbook for export, editing/creating records and replacement import, preserving dated backups.

## Tasks
1. Implement styled category XLSX export and validated import, including names/IDs for relations, new-row defaults and IDs, complete attachments and large-cell handling. Retain the old CSV reader for existing backups.
2. Update Settings/endpoints to XLSX; verify round trips, edited/new records, invalid workbooks and rollback on isolated databases. Restart the local app after verification.
3. Deliver the original local data in the new readable workbook without silently dropping retired distributor records. Document compatibility and results.

## Success criteria
Every category is a named sheet with serial numbers and real columns. Workbooks are editable and importable for their matching application schema. Attachments and data integrity survive round trips. Invalid imports preserve current data; dated backups remain. No live import or migrations during development.
