Import Data

Choose an Excel workbook (.xlsx) exported by this app (up to 100 MB). Edit values or add new rows in the category sheets. Stable identifiers are hidden; new rows receive IDs automatically. Import replaces business data across all category sheets together. Your owner login accounts and permissions are preserved.

Keep sheet names and column headers. Use unique matching names to link products, manufacturers and orders. The Read Me sheet explains dates, defaults and attachments.

You only upload this one business workbook. Before replacing anything, the app automatically saves a separate complete backup of your current records and attachments with today's date and time. Existing backups stay saved. Backup browsing will be added later.

After import, sign in with your existing owner account. If import fails, your current data stays unchanged.
