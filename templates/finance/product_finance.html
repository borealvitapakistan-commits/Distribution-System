Yellow columns come from your data: the batch's landed cost from its Manufacturer order, the product's retail price for that bottle size, and the quantity ordered. Totals of the money columns are per-unit figures × quantity. {% if product_id %}This product keeps only its own share of expenses, worked out across every batch.{% endif %} {% if not has_market %}The Market price column appears once you add Market Research.{% endif %}

How each column is worked out

  1. Manufacturer price = the batch's price × (1 + order tax %) + its share of the order's shipping (shared by value)
  2. Market price = average price per capsule of the Market Research rows marked "use" × our bottle size
  3. Distributor cut / unit = our retail price × {{ settings.distributor_cut_percentage|num }}%
  4. We receive / unit = our retail price − distributor cut
  5. Expense share % = this row's (we receive − manufacturer price) × quantity ÷ the same for all rows
  6. Expense / unit = expense share % × total expenses ({{ totals.expenses|num }}) ÷ quantity
  7. Profit / unit = we receive − manufacturer price − expense / unit
  8. Profit % = profit / unit ÷ our retail price
  9. Total profit = profit / unit × quantity
  10. Investment = Σ manufacturer price × quantity · Gross profit = Σ (we receive − manufacturer price) × quantity · Total profit = gross profit − expenses
